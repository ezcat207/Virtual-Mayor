# Competitors and adjacent products — Virtual Mayor

Research date: 2026-10-03. Method: web search + HTTP link check. Link status: OK = HTTP 200; "bot-blocked" = 403/406 to curl but page exists in search results; "unverified" = could not load.
Traffic numbers were NOT obtained (Similarweb needs login/paid); see how-to-learn-from-them.md. Figures marked (est.) come from third-party aggregators and are unreliable.

## Core finding
Nobody combines (a) a real named city's real data/budget/politics, (b) a guided mayor-role simulation with interviews, and (c) a map of that city's digital/AI governance. Games are generic or fictional; civic-ed is passive reading; AI-planning tools serve professionals and ignore learning/story; GovTech sells to governments, not individuals. The white space is real but the paying audience is unproven (see business-paths.md).
